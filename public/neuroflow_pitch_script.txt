[0:30 - 1:15] — LIVE PRODUCT DEMONSTRATION
(Visual action: Click 'Start Soundscape'. Move the master volume slider and adjust channel sliders)

"Let's look at the product in action.
First, notice there are zero audio files or pre-recorded loops. When I click 
'Start Soundscape', the browser synthesizes seven independent audio channels 
in real time:
- Brownian noise creates an acoustic cloak that completely isolates background speech.
- Rain and forest wind provide organic acoustic texture.
- And with headphones, our Binaural Beat Engine sends 211 Hz to the left ear 
  and 221 Hz to the right ear, creating a pure 10 Hz Alpha beat that synchronizes 
  brainwaves for relaxed alertness.

(Visual action: Hover your mouse across the canvas visualizer)
Right above the mixer, our 60 FPS harmonic canvas extracts frequency data 
in real time through an AnalyserNode, reacting organically as you move your cursor.
You can switch between Constellation, Aurora Waves, or full-screen Zen Mode."
